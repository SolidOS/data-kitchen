SolidOS Local

Your local pod, browsed with the SolidOS data browser — header, side-menu and outline view, exactly as if the local server used SolidOS as its front end. It opens at the pod root; click into folders and resources from there.

Logging in

There is nothing to log into locally: the local pod belongs to you, and the banner shows you signed in as its owner. Reads and writes ride Data Kitchen's own authenticated access to the pod.

To browse other pods with a real Solid login, use the SolidOS Remote plugin instead — the upstream SolidOS web app in its own window, with its own login button that works against any identity provider.

Why an iframe

SolidOS / mashlib carries a lot of CSS and JS that wants to own the whole page (a fixed banner, font and theme overrides, a global theme attribute on <html>). When mashlib was loaded directly into dk those rules bled into the Home and Podz pages — different theme, distorted layouts, coloured borders around panels. Confining SolidOS to an iframe keeps every one of those side effects inside its own document.
